Do coffee grounds really help plants grow?

EcoBean's coffee-ground fertilizer seemed to work, so I tested it properly: three pots, blind soil tests, and a daily log. It raised soil nutrients, but the water-only pot still grew the most. Now I'm working out why, with chemistry, as a Gold CREST Award project.
