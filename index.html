01 — About

I'm a high school student in Singapore, graduating in 2027. Most of what I do sits somewhere between business and service.

EcoBean started as a question about what happens to the aluminium cans and coffee grounds we throw away. It became a company that turns them into plant-growing kits, and finished third nationally in Junior Achievement Singapore. Project Wish came from wanting to do something concrete for people with limited time left, and now runs alongside Ambulance Wish Singapore and Make-A-Wish.

Alongside that: economics and physics, coding education for underprivileged children through Code For All, and coaching cricket to younger players on weekends.
